Independent portfolio materials covering digital account opening, customer onboarding, and ACH payment workflows. The documents demonstrate business analysis practice in requirements, user stories, business rules, exception flows, and UAT scenario design.


These are learning and design exercises based on generalized banking scenarios. They do not represent banking employment, client delivery, production implementation, or executed UAT. Thresholds and service levels in the documents are proposed scenario requirements, not measured results.

## Digital account opening and onboarding

It covers retail savings onboarding, customer and document capture, OCR, KYC/AML screening, core banking account creation, notifications, operational exceptions, and UAT scenario design.
